SEO Services Offered by Pavan Kumar

I provide complete SEO for businesses of every size. You can choose a single service or a full package, depending on where your website stands today.

Keyword Research and Strategy

Every successful SEO campaign starts with the right keywords. I study what your customers actually type into Google, how often they search, how competitive each term is and what they want to find. Then I group keywords by intent. Some people are only researching, while others are ready to call you today. A good strategy targets both groups, but gives priority to the keywords that lead to business. This research decides which pages your website needs, what each page should say and which terms deserve the most effort.

⚙️

Technical SEO

If search engines cannot crawl and understand your website properly, even great content will struggle. Technical SEO fixes the foundation. I check site speed, mobile friendliness, broken links, duplicate pages, redirect problems, sitemap and robots settings, indexing errors, structured data and Core Web Vitals. Many websites lose rankings because of problems their owners never see. Fixing them often creates the fastest early improvements, and it makes every later step more effective.

📝

On-Page SEO

On-page SEO is everything you control directly on your pages. I write and improve title tags, meta descriptions, headings, URLs, internal links, image descriptions and page content so that each page clearly matches the search it is meant to answer. The aim is natural, helpful writing that reads well for people and is understood easily by Google. I never stuff keywords into a page. Overusing a phrase makes content hard to read and can harm your rankings, so each page uses its main keyword and related terms only where they genuinely fit.

✍️

Content Writing and Optimization

Content is what gives Google a reason to rank you. I plan and create service pages, blog articles, landing pages and guides that answer real customer questions in a clear and trustworthy way. Existing pages can also be refreshed, expanded and restructured so they compete better. Good content builds authority, earns links naturally and keeps visitors on your site long enough to take action.

🔗

Off-Page SEO and Link Building

Backlinks from relevant, respected websites tell Google that your business deserves trust. I focus on quality over quantity, using safe and ethical methods such as relevant guest contributions, business listings, brand mentions and outreach. I avoid spammy link schemes because they can lead to penalties. The goal is stronger authority that lasts and grows over the long term.

📍

Local SEO

If you serve customers in a particular city or area, local SEO helps you appear when people search nearby. I optimize your Google Business Profile, local citations, reviews, location pages and map visibility. For shops, clinics, agencies, coaching centres and service providers, this can bring calls and visits from people who are close and ready to buy.

🛒

E-commerce SEO

Online stores face special challenges, such as thousands of product pages, similar descriptions and filter URLs. I optimize category pages, product pages, site structure, internal linking and product schema so your store attracts shoppers who are ready to purchase. Better rankings for product keywords mean more orders without depending only on paid advertising.

📊

Reporting and Conversion Tracking

You should never have to guess whether SEO is working. I track keyword positions, organic traffic, enquiries and conversions, then share clear updates in simple language. If something is not performing, I tell you and adjust the plan.

Common SEO Mistakes I Help Businesses Avoid

Many websites struggle not because the business is weak, but because of avoidable SEO mistakes. Targeting keywords that nobody searches for is one of the most common. Another is writing one page that tries to rank for everything, which leaves Google unsure what the page is about. Slow loading pages, poor mobile layouts and missing contact buttons also push visitors away before they ever become customers.

Other businesses buy cheap bulk backlinks, copy content from competitors or ignore Google Business Profile completely. These shortcuts may look attractive, but they waste money and can damage trust with search engines. I replace them with a clear, sustainable plan: the right keywords, helpful content, a fast and easy website, and quality links earned the right way.

The result is a website that works as a sales tool, not just an online brochure. Visitors find answers quickly, trust what they read and know exactly how to call or email you. That combination of strong visibility and easy contact is what turns search traffic into real business growth.

Frequently Asked Questions About SEO

How long does SEO take to show results?

Early improvements can appear in a few weeks, but meaningful ranking and traffic growth usually takes three to six months or more. Competitive industries may need longer. Consistent work is what produces lasting results.

Can you guarantee a number one ranking?

No honest SEO expert can guarantee a specific position because Google controls its own results. What I can promise is a proven, ethical process, transparent reporting and a sincere effort to move your important keywords toward the top three.

What is the difference between SEO and paid ads?

Ads give quick visibility but stop the moment you stop paying. SEO takes longer to build, but it keeps bringing traffic and enquiries without paying for every click, which often makes it more cost-effective over time.

Do you work with small and local businesses?

Yes. Local SEO is one of the fastest ways for a small business to get more calls and customers from its own area.

Is SEO a one-time job?

No. Search results, competitors and Google’s algorithms keep changing. Ongoing optimization protects your rankings and helps you keep improving.

How will I know that the SEO work is effective?

I share regular reports covering keyword rankings, organic traffic, enquiries and conversions. You will see clear progress and understand what each result means for your business.
